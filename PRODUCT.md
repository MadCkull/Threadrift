# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers build spatial stories and roadmaps; visitors explore their connected content using a mouse, trackpad, touch, or keyboard. Authors edit those maps in the embedded Studio.

## Product Purpose

Let people travel through a graph without freely panning a canvas. Success means fluid exploration with intentional, reliable route changes and content that stays readable.

## Brand Personality

Fluid, quiet, precise. These qualities follow the project's stated motion goals and the user's request for minimal, unobtrusive navigation.

## Anti-references

Mandatory decision dialogs at every fork, large persistent navigation cards obscuring the map, and controls that compete with the story.

## Design Principles

- Keep the map and its content primary.
- Continue naturally through a sensible default route.
- Let explicit user choices override automatic recommendations.
- Show route controls only when they can be used.
- Preserve strict rest and travelled-path guarantees.
- Let authors compose saved views independently of node placement while visitor travel remains anchored to the graph.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain labelled keyboard controls, visible focus, usable touch targets, and reduced-motion support from the accepted navigation plan. Icons need accessible names; route choices need destination text when expanded. No formal conformance level has been specified.
